DIMMING HISTORY

Direction changes the result.

At 17% command, HC measured about 12.5 lux ascending and 23.2 lux descending. Longer settling reduced some differences, but at 60% HC still differed by about 6% after 45 seconds.

Both directions remain visible in the graphs and downloads. These observations are an installation curve, not a universal specification for every fixture or dimmer.

FLICKER · READ WITH CARE

Variation is measured. Safety is not certified.

HC fast captures show a spectral candidate near 120 Hz. Observed min/max modulation was about 36% at full output and 40–42% at 50–60% command.

The sampling rate is only about 240–254 samples/second. Aliasing and phase coverage near its limit can understate modulation. Low-signal readings are quantization-limited; apparent zero or 100% modulation there is not a dependable severity estimate.

PstLM and SVM were not measured. HC visible flicker was not assessed. These graphs do not establish flicker-free operation.

METHOD & REPRODUCIBILITY

What the sensor actually saw.

Matched acquisition

HC: October 7, 2026. SD: October 6, 2026. Same fixed OPT4048 position and Lutron dimmer. A three-second settling wait, two color windows per setting, and a five-second fast waveform. Descent checks every 1% through 20%, then every 5% above.

Color & brightness

Color mode uses 25 ms conversions per channel and 30 individual samples per window. CCT is estimated from measured chromaticity using a nearest Planckian locus in CIE 1960 uv. No scaling to manufacturer ratings. Lux is illuminance at the sensor, not fixture lumens.

Limits & flags

Fast mode uses 600 μs conversions per channel. Modulation is 100 × (max−min)/(max+min), including sensor noise. The low-signal screen flags means below ten quantization steps. It is a measurement heuristic, not a flicker standard. Missing CCT is unavailable, never zero.
